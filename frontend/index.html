The Indian Road Safety Paradox
Why are absolute fatalities rising while individual vehicle safety is higher?
1. Asymmetric Growth: Registered motor vehicles expanded by 211x and paved road length grew by 5.1x, creating severe spatial congestion and mixed-speed conflicts.
2. Vehicle vs Roadside Safety: Modern cars protect occupants better (deaths per 10k vehicles dropped 95%), but vulnerable users (2-wheelers, pedestrians) bear 64% of lethal impacts.
3. Engineering Solution: Transitioning from passive vehicle safety to Safe System Infrastructure (IRC:103 pedestrian grade segregation and IRC:93 signalized junctions).
📌 Official Source: Ministry of Road Transport & Highways (MoRTH), Transport Research Wing, Govt. of India.
